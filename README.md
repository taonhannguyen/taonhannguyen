## The long version

The standard is older than the title.

**Computational chemistry.** PhD, Concordia University, 2005, with Gilles H. Peslherbe. Thesis: [*Theoretical studies of solvation and catalysis: clusters as chemical nano-reactors*](https://spectrum.library.concordia.ca/id/eprint/8643/). Monte Carlo, molecular dynamics, quantum chemistry — solvent clusters treated as reactors, not as a backdrop. The result I still cite is mechanical. Cluster impact drives oxygen into a reactive excited state because the Born–Oppenheimer approximation breaks down. The thesis names that failure *chemistry with a hammer*: [Physical Review Letters, 2006](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.97.143402), with Holger Vach and Peslherbe, and first author in [The Journal of Chemical Physics, 2011](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.3509772).

Before Concordia: Licence in physical chemistry, Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1.

**Markets.** 2006–2012. I designed and scripted systematic strategies on US markets. A model that does not survive execution is a story.

**Products.** President of Tekuno, 2012–2018. Native iOS and Android, shipped and kept in production — Pro Angler, Huddol, TrekFit, Specifik, and the Walter Abrasive, RADR, and Bio-Circle apps for Walter Surface Technologies, among a dozen others. At Appnovation I built and led the mobile practice: technical lead on the Looker mobile app, client-facing technical lead for CorePower Yoga, and front-end lead for Pfizer, Sesame Street, Fastenal, Visit California, and Tricida. Technical director at Onyx, 2021–2023: the full stack, and the team that owned it.

Write the mechanism. Compute it. Put a name on the result.
